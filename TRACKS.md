# Available Tracks
Theme: safety in motorsports

## Track 1: Safety Diagnosis
Build a tool that detects or identifies a safety risk in real time, or before it becomes critical.

## Track 2: Safety Testing
Build a tool that tests or simulates a safety system under stress and failure conditions.

## Track 3: Safety Fixing
Build a tool that directly addresses an identified safety issue.

## Supporting Companies

### Tangerine Track: Orange Flag
Whether managing a racing team, building a product, or serving clients, resources are never unlimited. Explore how technology can help motorsports teams go from R&D to the end of the last race of the season with money left in the bank.

### Ollon Track: Data-Driven Motorsport Safety
Build a solution that uses data to identify patterns, predict risks, or provide insights that could improve driver or vehicle safety in motorsport.

### TELUS Track: Best Connected Solution
Build something that gets smarter, safer, or more useful because it's connected.

### Ampire Track: AI for Motorsport Safety
Challenging students to develop an Al-powered solution that detects, predicts, or prevents safety risks in motorsport
